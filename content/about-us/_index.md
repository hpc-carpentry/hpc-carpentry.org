HPC Carpentry lowers the barrier to high-performance computing operations for a wide range of users, so that more people can benefit from the growing availability of increasingly sophisticated computer systems. 
We develop and deliver workshops teaching the effective and responsible use of shared, distributed computer systems.
These workshops are aimed at researchers and data scientists who need to access HPC environments and execute tasks on those systems to advance their projects.

Modern research is increasingly computationally intensive, while access to more capable computational resources is becoming more widespread. 
For many researchers, this can create a skills gap, where the expertise needed to make effective use of these resources and execute computational research workflows is missing. 
This gap can be widened by the lack of computational instruction in researchers' formal backgrounds, as well as by the fast-moving nature of the computational landscape.

Our project addresses this gap using the same workshop model and pedagogy that has been successful in closing related gaps in other Carpentries lesson programs. 

Since 2018, HPC Carpentry has been teaching people the foundational knowledge and skills necessary to access HPC systems and use them efficiently and responsibly. 
Our hands-on workshops teach data scientists and researchers to connect to a remote HPC system, transfer data between the cluster and their personal computers, and manage the execution of processes across its resources.
After participating in one of our workshops, learners will be ready to migrate their analysis workflows to a high-performance computing environment and scale them up there, request appropriate resources for their tasks, and act as "responsible citizens" when working on shared computing systems.

Although HPC Carpentry workshops are relatively short and cannot teach everything there is to know about working in an HPC environment, our lessons equip learners with the skills, conceptual understanding, and confidence needed to continue learning and engaging effectively with HPC systems. 
Ultimately, our learners will be empowered to take full advantage of the resources available to them, scaling up research and advancing their fields through efficient, reproducible, and responsible computing practices.
