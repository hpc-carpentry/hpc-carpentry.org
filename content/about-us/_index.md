HPC Carpentry lowers the barrier to high-performance computing operations for a wide range of users, so that more people can benefit from the growing availability of increasingly sophisticated computer systems. 
We develop and deliver workshops teaching effective and responsible use of shared, distributed compute systems.
The target audience of these workshops is researchers and data scientists who need to access HPC environments and execute tasks on those systems to advance their projects.

Data is produced and published in increasing size and complexity, leading to a growing need for large-scale analyses that often have specialised hardware and software requirements.
Many research institutions and data-driven organisations have developed access to powerful, shared computing environments that allow their members to fulfill the needs of their data processing and analysis pipelines.
An ability to harness these sophisticated computing resources empowers individuals to scale up their research and analyses, unlocking a range of methods and a magnitude of processing that were hitherto unavailable.
However, many researchers do not receive routine training in these skills and the conceptual differences between HPC systems and personal computer platforms present a barrier to adoption among novices.

Since 2018, HPC Carpentry has been teaching people the foundational knowledge and skills necessary to access HPC systems and use them efficiently and responsibly. 
Our hands-on workshops teach data scientists and researchers to connect to a remote HPC system, transfer data between this cluster and their personal computer, and manage the execution of processes across the resources on the cluster.
After participating in one of our workshops, learners will be ready to migrate and scale up their analysis workflows onto a high-performance computing environment, request an adequate and appropriate allocation of resources for their tasks, and act as "responsible citizens" working on shared computing systems.

Although HPC Carpentry workshops are relatively short and cannot teach everything there is to know about working in an HPC environment, our lessons equip learners with the skills, conceptual understanding, and confidence needed for continued learning and engagement. 
Our ultimate goal is to enable learners to take full advantage of the resources that are available to them, using efficient, reproducible, and responsible computing practices.
