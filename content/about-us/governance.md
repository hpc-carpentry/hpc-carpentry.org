The HPC Carpentry Governance Committee provides community leadership for the lesson program, with responsibility to oversee project strategy, advocate for HPC Carpentry and its community, and to communicate with the governance of The Carpentries, the Core Team, and the Carpentries community as a whole.

See the [Data Carpentry](https://datacarpentry.org/about-us/governance/) Governance page as an example.  The source file for this view will be `data/governance.yaml`
