## Steering Committee

HPC Carpentry is guided by a Steering Committee that provides strategic direction,
supports the development and sustainability of the community and its lessons, and
helps shape curriculum development in accordance with The Carpentries Handbook.
The committee also represents HPC Carpentry in discussions with The Carpentries,
supporting the programme's progression towards formal recognition as an official
Lesson Program.
